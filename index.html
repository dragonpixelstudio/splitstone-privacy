Privacy Policy · Effective September 6, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how the mobile game Splitstone handles information. It applies to versions distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Splitstone does not require a Dragon Pixel Studio account, and game progress is stored locally on your device. Available advertising, consent, sign-in, leaderboard, and achievement services may vary by platform.

Information stored on your device

Splitstone stores game data locally, including language and audio settings, tutorial status, progress, best scores, unlocked achievements, and daily-challenge progress. This information is not sent to Dragon Pixel Studio. It remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to Android or iOS backup and restore settings.

Google Mobile Ads

Splitstone uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and standard interstitial ads. It does not use rewarded, rewarded-interstitial, app-open, or playable-specific ad formats.

Depending on your region and consent choices, Google may collect or receive:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, Android app set ID, or Apple advertising identifier where available and permitted;
  • app and ad interactions; and
  • diagnostic information used for analytics, security, fraud prevention, and service operation.

Google's SDK transmits data using encryption in transit. Where required, Splitstone displays Google's consent form before requesting ads and provides a privacy-options control when Google requires one. On iOS, access to Apple's advertising identifier is subject to the device's tracking-permission setting. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Google Play Games Services

On supported Android devices, Splitstone uses Google Play Games Services for optional platform sign-in, a high-score leaderboard, and achievements. To provide and protect those services, Google may collect or process your Play Games player identifier, gamer identity and profile name, account email address where discoverable, approximate country or region, device identifiers, game scores, achievement status, and related game activity or diagnostics. What other players can see depends on your Google Play Games profile and privacy settings. Dragon Pixel Studio does not receive your Google account password or directly receive your account email address through this integration.

You can manage the Play Games profile used for Splitstone in your Android and Google Play Games settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

Apple Game Center

On supported iOS devices, Splitstone may use Apple Game Center for optional platform sign-in, leaderboards, and achievements. When you use Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center player identifier, nickname or profile information, scores, achievement status, device information, and related activity or diagnostics. What other players can see depends on your Apple and Game Center privacy settings. Dragon Pixel Studio does not receive your Apple Account password.

You can manage Game Center and its visibility in your iOS settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's data practices.

App stores and platform services

Google Play and the Apple App Store may independently process information related to downloading, installing, updating, securing, or diagnosing Splitstone. That processing is governed by your platform provider's terms, privacy policy, and account settings.

Information we do not collect

Dragon Pixel Studio does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, messages, photos, files, precise location, health information, financial information, or payment information. Google or Apple may process the limited account and profile information described above when you use their platform services. Splitstone has no in-app purchases, chat, user-generated content, or Dragon Pixel Studio account system.

Sharing and retention

Dragon Pixel Studio does not sell personal information. Data used by Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Games Services, Apple Game Center, and the applicable app store is handled by Google or Apple to provide those services, subject to their terms, retention practices, and your settings. Local game data is retained on your device as described above.

Children

Splitstone is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Your choices

  • Use the in-app privacy-options control when available to review advertising consent choices.
  • Manage or reset advertising identifiers and, on iOS, review tracking permission in your device settings.
  • Manage your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center profile and visibility in the applicable platform settings.
  • Clear Splitstone's storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored game data.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. Google and Apple may process information on servers outside your country. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Splitstone's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@dragonpixelstudio.com.
